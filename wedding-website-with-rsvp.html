How to make a wedding website with RSVP, without an app or a monthly fee

Most couples need one page, not a whole website: the date, where to go, what happens when, and an easy way to reply. Here is how to make that in about fifteen minutes.

What a wedding website really needs

Guests open your link on their phone, usually from WhatsApp. They want five things, in this order:

  1. Your names and the date, clearly at the top.
  2. When and where: a start time, the venue and a map button.
  3. The order of the day: ceremony, drinks, dinner, first dance.
  4. An RSVP that takes under a minute.
  5. The practical bits: dress code, where to stay, gifts, a short FAQ.

Everything else is optional. A short page that loads fast beats a long site guests scroll past.

Step 1 · Write your details first

Before you open any tool, write down your names, date, start time, venue address, three to five moments for the schedule, and how you want replies: WhatsApp, email or a Google Form. This takes ten minutes and makes the rest easy.

Step 2 · Choose how guests reply

MethodGood forWatch out for
WhatsAppFast replies, guests already use itAdd a backup email for guests without WhatsApp
EmailOlder guests, formal weddingsReplies can land in spam, so check it
Google FormLarge weddings, meal choices in a spreadsheetOne more link for guests to open

Step 3 · Add the venue's time zone

If any guests live abroad, set the countdown and calendar to the venue's time zone. Otherwise a guest in London sees the wrong start time for a wedding in Dubai.

Step 4 · Share one link and a QR code

Send the link on WhatsApp, email or text, and print the QR code on your save the date or paper invitation. Keep it short: a link with a long schedule makes a denser QR code.

Step 5 · Keep your guest list private

Many wedding sites store your guest list on their servers. Our maker packs your details inside the link itself and sends replies straight to you, so there is no account and no database.

Questions

Do I need to pay monthly for a wedding website?

No. Many builders charge monthly or show ads. A one-time maker gives you a link that keeps working, with no subscription.

Can guests RSVP without downloading an app?

Yes. Guests open the link in their phone browser and reply by WhatsApp, email or your Google Form.

Can I change details after sending?

Yes. Make a new link with the change and send it again. Old links keep the old details.
