WhatsApp RSVP for weddings: collect replies in one tap

Guests answer WhatsApp messages faster than emails or cards. Here is how to get clean, complete RSVPs without chasing anyone.

Why WhatsApp works for RSVPs

Most guests open WhatsApp many times a day. A reply there takes seconds, and you see it straight away. The problem is messy answers: "we'll come!" with no names or meal choice.

The fix: a pre-written reply

Give guests a short form on your invitation page. When they tap Send, WhatsApp opens with a neat message already written, like:

RSVP — Amelia & James
Name: Sara Ahmed
Attending: Yes
Guests: 2
Guest names: Omar Ahmed
Dietary needs: Vegetarian

Every reply arrives in the same format, so counting guests takes minutes, not evenings.

What to ask (and what to skip)

  • Ask: name, attending yes or no, number of guests, guest names, dietary needs.
  • Optional: one extra question, such as a meal choice or song request.
  • Skip: long forms. Every extra question means fewer replies.

Set a reply-by date

Put a clear date on the invitation, about four to six weeks before the wedding. Caterers usually need final numbers two to three weeks ahead.

Plan for guests without WhatsApp

Add a backup email, and a "copy my reply" option so guests can send it any way they like.

Questions

Will my number be visible to guests?

Yes, the reply goes to the WhatsApp number you add, so guests will see it. Use a number you are happy to share with guests.

Can I track replies in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Because every reply has the same format, you can paste them into a simple guest list sheet.

What if a guest declines?

They choose "Regretfully declines" and the guest fields are hidden, so the reply stays short and kind.
